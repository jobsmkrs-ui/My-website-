MK Technologies, short for Machine Knowledge Resilience Solutions (Adaptive Network for Technology and Automation), exists to make business software resilient and owner-controlled. We believe organizations should never lose access to their systems or their data because of a connection, a subscription or a vendor decision. That belief shaped AntA: software that works offline, protects data locally and uses online intelligence only when you choose.

From clinics and retail stores to vehicle fleets, HR departments, private homes and rental businesses, AntA turns day-to-day operations into organized, trackable and measurable workflows, with no hidden fees and no artificial limits. Every AntA solution runs locally first, then connects online when you want intelligence, remote access or integration — never the other way around.

Mission

To give businesses and individuals software they fully own: their code, their data and their operations, protected by offline security and empowered by online intelligence.

Vision

A region where every clinic, shop, fleet, HR team, home and rental business runs on resilient systems that never depend on someone else's servers to keep working.
